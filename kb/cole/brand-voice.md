# Acme brand voice

## Tone
Clear, concrete, no hype. We write like an engineer explaining something to a peer:
short sentences, specific nouns, no superlatives. Never use "revolutionary",
"game-changing", or "seamless".

## Launch posts
Every launch post must include the registration link above the fold, name the date and
timezone explicitly, and end with a single call to action. Launch posts are capped at
250 words.

## Claims
Any statistic must name its source inline. If we cannot source a number, we cut it
rather than soften it.
